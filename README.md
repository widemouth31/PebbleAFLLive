Simple Pebble Watch App to display current Australian Football League scores on your Pebble Watch. Games in Progress or all Current Round Games can be selected via Settings or by long pressing the watch middle Button. 

Configurable Auto-Refresh time in App Settings.

Single Press Middle Button - Refresh scores.
Long Press Middle Button - Toggle display of current, or all round games.
